Play a little. Learn a lot.

Three short games about AI, made by your neighbors. There are no timers and nothing to sign up for. Take your time, and play as often as you like.

Scam or safe?

Scammers now use AI to sound more convincing. Read each message, then choose. You’ll see the clues after every answer.

Match the words

Choose a word, then choose the meaning that goes with it. A wrong guess costs nothing, so go ahead and try.

Ask it better

An AI helper can only work with what you tell it. Add details to the question, watch out for anything too private, then send it.

Have a real question about AI or a message you’re not sure about? Chat with Cindy AI or ask Howie, a real person. The messages and AI answers in these games are examples written for practice.
