Meet Howie — Real Person, Real Answers

👤 100% Human  ·  Not AI

Not everyone is ready to ask a computer. That's okay — Howie gets it. At 85, he's spent decades teaching and sharing knowledge about technology, AI, assistive devices, and resources for older adults and people with disabilities.

Whether you have a question about AI, need help finding local resources, or just want a human perspective on technology — Howie is here. Submit your question below and Howie will respond personally.
