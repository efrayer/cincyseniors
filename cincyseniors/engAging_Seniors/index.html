Community Course

Free Smartphone Basics for Beginners

A short, supportive 3-week course designed for older adults who want to learn smartphone skills at a comfortable pace — with real, hands-on help every step of the way.

📞 Call to Register — (513) 564-9777

📚 Skills You'll Learn

  • Foundations of smartphone use and basic mechanics
  • Phone care and maintenance for everyday reliability
  • Security and safety basics to protect your information
  • Using Settings to adapt your smartphone to your personal needs

📋 Course Details

📅 Length: 3 weeks — one session per week on Mondays
🌱 Level: Beginner — we start at the very beginning
🤝 Format: In-person, hands-on instruction
📱 Devices: iPhone or Android purchased after January 2020
👥 Class size: Small classes — one Senior Guide per 2 participants
📖 Materials: Guided, step-by-step beginner's manual included

About EngAging Seniors

EngAging Seniors is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that provides Mobile Digital Literacy (MDL) skill development and experiential opportunities that empower older adults to participate effectively in today's increasingly technological world.
