About This Team

The CaiC Older Adults team is dedicated to ensuring that AI benefits every generation. We believe older adults deserve access to practical, plain-language guidance on how AI can support daily life — from health and wellness to finances, social connection, and safety online.

Our community hub is CincySeniors.org — a free, no-signup resource built specifically for older adults and caregivers in Greater Cincinnati.

Meet Cindy AI

Cindy is CincySeniors.org's AI guide — trained on local Cincinnati senior resources, programs, and services. Ask her anything in plain English: no signup, no login required.

About CincySeniors.org

CincySeniors.org is a free, community-run website created to help older adults across Greater Cincinnati learn about AI safely — no ads, no sales, no signup required.

It is maintained in partnership with the CaiC Older Adults team and includes AI-powered tools, local resource directories, educational guides, and a human question-and-answer service.
