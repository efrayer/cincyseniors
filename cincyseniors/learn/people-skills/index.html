PEOPLE SKILLS
AI CAN'T REPLACE

How work is changing, and the human strengths to encourage and hire

Ideas drawn from Noema Magazine

Compiled by Howie Baum and Claude AI

📄 Download PDF
Slide 2
Context

Why This Matters Now

101,743
US job cuts that employers blamed on AI, January to June 2026
16%
Drop in jobs for workers aged 22 to 25 in AI-exposed fields
57%
Share of Claude conversations where people thought with AI rather than handing work off

Sources: Challenger, Gray & Christmas; Stanford Digital Economy Lab; Anthropic Economic Index (cited by Friedland, Noema)

Slide 3
The Shift

Keyboard Work Shrinks, Judgment Grows

⬇ Shrinking

  • Drafting routine documents
  • Entering and moving data
  • Writing routine code
  • First-draft reports

⬆ Growing

  • Judgment when things are messy
  • Sorting signal from noise
  • Persuading and negotiating
  • Building trust with people
Slide 4
Historical Context

History's Lesson: Tools Reshape Expertise

Before 1800
Artisans

Skilled craftsmen made each item by hand

1800s–1950s
Factories

Machines created new skilled jobs: machinists, electricians, clerks

1960s–2020
Computers

Routine office jobs faded; pay rose mostly for college-educated experts

Now
AI

Could help more workers make expert decisions

Slide 5
Research Evidence

AI Helps Those Who Know Their Work

40%
Less time on writing tasks, and the weakest writers caught up to average
3 vs 10
Months for new customer service agents to match experienced ones
No gain
For radiologists who weren't trained to use the AI tool well
"Like a nail gun, AI helps the trained worker and endangers the beginner."
Slide 6
Rising Skills

The Judgment Economy: 3 Rising Skills

🤝

Bringing People Together

Persuading, building agreement, leading.

Example: A supply chain director who convinces partners to act on the AI's plan
🔍

Interpreting Information

Turning data into wise choices and risk calls.

Example: A fund manager weighing how a crisis changes a 20-year plan
🧩

Connecting Ideas

Combining insights from many fields.

Example: A transit planner blending health, climate and community needs
Slide 7
Human Strengths

What People Bring That AI Does Not

❤️
Lived experience We have felt grief, hunger, fear and joy
🤲
Presence We can show up and stay with someone
✅
Trust and accountability We answer for our decisions
🧭
Moral judgment We weigh right and wrong, not just odds
👁️
Reading the room We sense the unspoken and the mood
💡
Original creativity We link unlike ideas in new ways
⏳
Sense of history We know why things came to be
💬
Persuasion We negotiate and win people over
🔧
Hands-on know-how We handle the messy physical world
🎯
Purpose and caring We care how things turn out
Slide 8
Workplace Practice

Thinking Skills to Encourage at Work

❓
Question the output Is it accurate? What is missing? Who checked it?
🎯
Ask "so what?" Which finding actually matters for our goals?
🔗
Connect across fields What can we learn from another field or from history?
📣
Explain it plainly Can a busy director grasp it in two minutes?
🧠
Know your own thinking When should I trust the AI, and when trust myself?
Slide 9
Practical Framework

From AI Findings to Clear Answers: 4 Steps

1
Check

Verify facts and numbers against a trusted source

2
Choose

Pick the 2 or 3 findings that matter most

3
Translate

Say what it means in plain words

4
Advise

Suggest a next step and name the risks

Example

"The AI says visits rose 18% since spring. I checked our sign-in sheets and it's right. Most growth is seniors on Saturdays. I recommend adding two Saturday volunteers. The risk is a volunteer shortage in December."

Slide 10
The Research

Costly Mistake: Speed Isn't Good Thinking

AI simply added on

  • Doctors with AI: no more accurate
  • Drug sales: about 20% below no AI at all
  • Consultants: worse on judgment tasks

People and AI as a team

  • Doctors: accuracy rose from 75% to 82–85%
  • Drug sales: up 16%, with 40% more client meetings
  • Consultants: 40% better on tasks AI handles well
"Same AI. The difference was how the work was set up."
Slide 11
Design Principle

Keep People in the Driver's Seat

Person leads, AI assists

Judgment stays sharp and keeps growing

AI leads, person follows

People "fall asleep at the wheel" and skills fade

A Real Reversal

In 2024, Swedish company Klarna replaced hundreds of customer service staff with AI chatbots. By 2025, service had slipped and it rehired people.

Slide 12
Hiring & Management

Hiring for the AI Era: What to Look For

Old focus Add this focus
Speed at typing and data entry Good decisions under uncertainty
One narrow technical skill Curiosity across many fields
Degree or job title Track record of real decisions
Working alone fast Teamwork, trust and persuasion
Knowing the answers Asking good questions and checking AI
Interview idea: Hand over an AI summary. Ask what they would check and say.
Slide 13
Leadership Actions

How Leaders Encourage These Strengths

📋

Protect learning

Keep entry-level work that teaches judgment; pair new staff with mentors

🏆

Reward good decisions

Praise sound calls and lessons learned, not just volume

👥

Design teamwork with AI

Have people and AI assess separately, then compare

🎓

Train, then trust

Teach staff when to rely on AI and when to override it

Slide 14
Honest Concerns

Honest Risks to Watch

⬇️

Lower-paid replacement jobs

Good jobs could be refilled with poorly paid ones

1️⃣

A missing bottom rung

Fewer entry-level jobs means fewer ways to gain experience

⚖️

Gains for a few

Profits could concentrate with whoever owns the AI

🏛️

Unequal access

Broad education and training aren't available to everyone

Slide 15
Policy & Action

Sharing the Gains Early

The big idea

Give people skills and a stake early, not just checks after layoffs

Model: the 1944 GI Bill paid for school, homes and business loans

It helped build America's largest middle class

Help available now

  • Free retraining for laid-off workers through WIOA
  • In Ohio: local OhioMeansJobs centers
  • RAISE US: $500M+ effort testing wage insurance and retraining
Slide 16

AI can interpret my grief. It cannot grieve.

— Houda Nait El Barj

Invest in judgment, presence and trust.
They are what make AI worth using.
