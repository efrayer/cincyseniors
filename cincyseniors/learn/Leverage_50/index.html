Free Learning Community

Leverage AI 50+

A free online community created specifically to help adults 50+ learn about and confidently use AI — no technical background needed.

🚀

Join the Free Community

Access videos, prompts, training, practical examples, resources, discussions, and ongoing education around AI — all in one welcoming place.

What's Included

Everything You Need to Get Started

Start wherever you are and learn at your own pace.

🎥

Videos & Training

Clear, step-by-step videos and training modules designed for adults 50+ who are new to AI — or just want to go deeper.

💬

Community & Discussion

Ask questions, share experiences, and learn alongside others in a friendly, supportive community of people just like you.

📋

Prompts & Practical Examples

Ready-to-use AI prompts and real-world examples so you can immediately apply what you learn to work, hobbies, and everyday life.

🔒

Privacy & Safety Education

Learn how to use AI responsibly, including guidance on privacy, security, and navigating the rapidly changing AI landscape.

📈

Ongoing Learning

New resources and education added regularly so you stay current as AI continues to evolve — without feeling overwhelmed.

Our Philosophy

You Don't Have to Be a Techie

"AI, explained by neighbors… at your own pace."

People can start where they are, learn at their own pace, and discover practical ways AI can support their work, businesses, families, hobbies, creativity, productivity, and everyday lives. Our goal isn't simply to teach people about AI — it's to help older adults develop the confidence, knowledge, and skills to participate in an increasingly AI-powered world. — Leverage AI 50+
Who It's For

Built for Adults 50 and Older

Whether you've never touched AI or you're curious to do more, this community meets you where you are.

👔

Work & Business

Discover how AI can boost productivity, support your business, and help you stay competitive in the modern workplace.

🎨

Hobbies & Creativity

Explore how AI can enhance writing, art, music, research, and the creative pursuits you love.

🏡

Everyday Life

Use AI to simplify tasks, answer questions, plan trips, manage information, and make daily life a little easier.
